05 Working together

How I
work

A simple, professional process—so you always know what happens next and when files land in your inbox.

Brief & script

Share your script, reference tone, and deadline. English briefs are fine—I record in Tamil.

Custom audition

Optional short audition on your lines so you can approve tone before the full session.

Record & direction

Directed session in my home studio, with feedback rounds via email or WhatsApp.

Delivery

Clean, edited files—typically within 24–72 hours depending on length and revisions.

Home studio · condenser mic · treated booth · quiet environment for broadcast-ready takes.

Languages

Primary delivery in Tamil; English sessions when required.

File formats
